HEMS Service Overview

The specialized field of Helicopter Emergency Medical Services (HEMS) in Western Pennsylvania is characterized by the complex operations of major providers and a deep commitment to advanced training supported by high-fidelity simulation technologies. This environment encompasses leading healthcare networks, highly reliable helicopter platforms, and evolving safety standards designed to mitigate the inherent risks of critical care air transport.

The primary HEMS landscape in the region involves services provided by two major healthcare systems: Allegheny Health Network (AHN) and the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center (UPMC).

Allegheny Health Network (AHN) LifeFlight

Allegheny Health Network (AHN) operates LifeFlight, which offers regional emergency helicopter and critical care ground transportation services, available 24 hours day, seven days a week, for critically ill and injured patients needing immediate specialized care,. AHN is a non-profit, 14-hospital academic medical system headquartered in Pittsburgh, supported by its parent company, Highmark Health. 

  • Service Area and Scale: LifeFlight serves a four-state area that encompasses western Pennsylvania, southeastern Ohio, northern West Virginia, and western Maryland. The organization maintains a significant operational history, having completed over 75,000 missions while working closely with EMS providers and community hospitals.
  • Personnel and Facilities: The LifeFlight team includes specialized personnel such as flight nurses, flight paramedics, pilots, mechanics, communications specialists, and administrative support personnel. Key AHN facilities often involved in these transports include Allegheny General Hospital (the flagship), West Penn Hospital, and Wexford Hospital, alongside numerous specialized institutes and outpatient centers,,.

Stat MedEvac

 STAT MedEvac is another prominent provider in the region, operating as a service of the Center for Emergency Medicine of Western Pennsylvania,. This organization is directed by a consortium of UPMC hospitals, including UPMC Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh, UPMC Altoona, UPMC Hamot, UPMC Mercy, and UPMC Presbyterian Shadyside,

  • Scale and Scope: STAT MedEvac is recognized as one of the largest single-operated and dispatched air-medical transport systems in the United States,,. It maintains 18 helicopter base sites spanning Pennsylvania, Maryland, New York, Ohio, and the District of Columbia,.
  • Logistics and Coordination: The organization operates a three-department communications center, processing approximately 22,000 requests for medical service each year throughSTATCOM. Additionally,  STAT MedEvac operates ground ambulances for patients with severe injury or critical illness,,
